# Statistical Methods for Composite Endpoints
## Win Ratio and Beyond

By Lu Mao. [Read the book website](https://lmaowisc.github.io/ce/).

The materials grew out of a short course taught at the 2024 Annual Meeting of the Society for Clinical Trials (SCT).

Editable book chapters are in [`website/`](website/); see the [editing guide](website/EDITING.md). Build with `python website/build.py`. Generated pages are published from `docs/`.

The original root Quarto companion notes, R analyses, slide sources, and existing slide presentations are retained as course material. The book chapters are being developed from these sources.
